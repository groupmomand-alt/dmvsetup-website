Event Setup and Teardown in Washington, DC

Your event, set up right and taken down when it is over.

DMV Setup Squad builds the room before your event and clears it after. We set up booths, staging, screens, tables, chairs, and signage for conferences, trade shows, corporate events, holiday markets, fundraisers, and company parties across Washington, DC.

What we set up

Trade show booths

Booth walls, backdrops, tables, and product displays, built to your floor plan.

Staging

Stage decks, steps, and platforms for speakers, panels, and performances.

Screens and AV

TV screens and monitors mounted and placed, with cables kept neat and out of the way.

Tables and chairs

Banquet, classroom, and theater layouts, set to your seating plan.

Signage and wayfinding

Banners, signs, and direction boards placed where guests can see them.

Who we work with

Conference and trade show organizers

Exhibitors who need their booth built and taken down

Companies running corporate events, product launches, and holiday parties

Market organizers and fundraisers

If your event needs hands on site, we are the crew.

How it works

1. Send us the job details

Tell us the venue, the date, the hours, and what needs to be built. Photos or a floor plan help.

2. Get your free quote

We will send you a quote shortly.

3. We set up

Our crew works around your load-in time and has the room ready before doors open.

4. We tear down

After the event, we take it all down and leave the space clear.

Towns we serve

Washington, DC, including downtown, the Convention Center area, Capitol Hill, Georgetown, and Navy Yard. We also work across Arlington, Alexandria, Bethesda, Rockville, Silver Spring, Fairfax, and Germantown.

Frequently asked questions

Do you work with the venue's load-in schedule?

Yes. Tell us your load-in and load-out times and we plan around them.

Can you set up and tear down on the same day?

Yes. Most events are set up in the morning and torn down the same night.

Do you bring your own tools?

Yes. Our crew brings the tools needed for the job.

What do you need from me before the job?

The venue address, the date and hours, a list of what needs to be set up, and a floor plan or photos if you have them.

Do you work at the Walter E. Washington Convention Center?

Yes. We set up booths and staging for conferences and trade shows there and at other DC venues.
