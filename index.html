Common questions

How much does it cost?

Every job is different. Call, text or fill the form — tell us what you need and we give you a free, clear quote before you book. No obligation.

What are your hours?

Every day, 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM. Early mornings and late evenings work well for businesses that want to avoid downtime during regular hours.

Do I need to supply anything?

Not usually. We bring the tools and hardware for TV mounting and furniture assembly. For packing jobs, we can bring supplies — just ask when you request a quote.

Can I book more than one service in one visit?

Yes. Many customers book packing and moving help together, or moving help and furniture assembly. Tell us what you need and we build one plan and one price.

Do you work with businesses and events?

Yes. We handle office moves, office furniture assembly, trade-show booth setup and take-down, and corporate event setup. We work early mornings and evenings to fit around your schedule and keep downtime low.

Can you set up our trade-show booth?

Yes. Tell us the show name, venue, dates and your booth size. We check the venue's rules before we confirm, since some convention centers have restrictions on who can set up booths.

Where do you work?

Across the DMV: Washington DC, Maryland and Virginia. Not sure if we cover your area? Call or text us at (202) 505-3133.

How do I pay?

We discuss payment when we give you your quote. We keep it simple and clear.
